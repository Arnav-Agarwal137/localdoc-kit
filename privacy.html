LocalDoc Kit is a page that runs in your phone's web browser. It converts, shrinks, reads and edits your files on your own phone. We do not run a server that receives your files or your details, so we cannot see, keep, sell or share them. This policy explains exactly what the app does touch, so you can check it.

01Your files

Files you choose are read and rebuilt inside your browser, on your phone. They are not uploaded and the app does not store them; when you close the page they are gone from it. The only exceptions are the two tools in section 03, which tell you before they send anything.

02What is kept on your phone

A few things are remembered in your browser's own storage on your phone, never sent to us:

  • Your details, only if you save them (name, parents' names, date of birth, gender, address, PIN code, phone, email, category, blood group and, if you type it, your Aadhaar number), to fill forms for you. They are kept as plain text in this browser until you tap Forget them. Anyone who can use your phone's browser could read them, so save them only on a phone you control.
  • AI keys for Google Gemini or Groq, only if you tick remember.
  • Word lists made for a book (clear them with Forget the saved word lists), and your reading place and text size in the Book reader.
  • The speech model, once downloaded, so it need not be downloaded again.

Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes all of it.

03When the app uses the internet

  • Downloading its own tools. The first time you use a bigger tool (a PDF reader, the text recogniser, a speech or translation model), the app downloads that tool from public software hosts: jsDelivr, cdnjs, PyPI (files.pythonhosted.org) and Hugging Face. Nothing from your files is sent. These hosts, like any website, see your device's internet (IP) address when it asks them for a file.
  • "Is this book free?" sends the book title you type to Open Library, Wikidata and Google Books, to look the book up. If you paste in your own Google Books key, it goes only to Google Books with that look-up, and is kept in this browser only if you tick “Remember it”.
  • "Book words" sends parts of a book's text to Google Gemini, or to Groq, only if you paste in your own key and tick "Yes, send this book's text" for that book.

Those services' own privacy policies apply to what they receive. The app shows a counter that names every internet request made after you choose a file, so you can see this for yourself. The single-file copy of the app fetches nothing at all when it opens.

04Phone permissions

  • Microphone: only when you tap Start the microphone; the speech is turned into text on your phone.
  • Camera: only through your phone's own camera, when you choose to take a photo as a file.
  • Clipboard: the app can copy text to it when you tap a copy button; it does not read it.

The app does not use your location, contacts, notifications or anything else on the phone.

05Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, children included. A child's parent or guardian should decide whether the child saves details in the browser (section 02).

06Health tools

The prescription, lab report and medicine leaflet tools give general educational information only. They are not a medical device and not medical advice. They work on your phone and send nothing.

07Your rights, and India's law

Because we collect and process no personal data, there is nothing held by us to access, correct or delete under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or any similar law. Everything the app keeps is on your phone and in your control, as section 02 describes.
